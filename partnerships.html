FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Partnership Program FAQ

Answers to common questions about our partnership program

How quickly do I get paid for referrals?

For the flat fee model, you'll receive payment within 30 days of the merchant's first transaction. For the residual model, payments are distributed by the 15th of each month for the previous month's processing.

What's the typical approval process for partners?

After submitting your application, our partnership team will review it within 1-2 business days. If approved, you'll receive onboarding materials and can begin referring merchants immediately.

Do I need payment processing experience?

No experience is required. We provide comprehensive training and marketing materials to help you understand our solutions and effectively present them to your clients.

How are referrals tracked?

Each partner receives a unique referral code and access to our partner portal. All referrals are tracked through this system, ensuring you receive proper credit for every merchant you bring to Solara.

Can I switch between compensation models?

Yes, partners can choose to switch compensation models after a minimum period of 6 months. Any existing merchant accounts will remain under the original agreement terms.

What happens if a referred merchant cancels?

For flat fee partners, there's no impact as payment has already been made. For residual partners, earnings will cease for that specific merchant, but all other merchant accounts remain unaffected.
